Public-source monitoring

The prototype monitors selected public sources for material, date-linked changes. It reports source-supported facts and flags missing information rather than guessing.

Current data gaps

Good reporting makes missing evidence visible. These items must be confirmed before any operational, carbon, diversion, or partnership claim is made.

Operational records

No private food-scrap weights, invoices, pickup tickets, photos, or receipts have been provided by participating businesses.

Diversion confirmation

A public facility listing does not prove that any named business sends material to that facility or has an active arrangement.

Carbon / ESG calculations

No verified emissions-reduction claim can be made without actual weights, a confirmed destination, and a documented calculation method.
